Budget and housing · Example Munich

€1,200 warm
in Munich.
Furnished.

Munich asking rents average around €23 per m² cold (Kiel Institute, Q2 2026). Our clear recommendation is a furnished apartment: your colleague arrives with suitcases, not furniture, and furnished rentals usually come all-in with internet and electricity. At €1,200 all-in that is a well-cut studio or 1.5 rooms with a work corner.

A
Furnished, all-in

Bed, kitchen, desk, internet and electricity included. Your colleague moves in on landing day: no furniture shopping, no six-week wait for an internet line, no hotel.

B
Inside the city

Moosach, Milbertshofen, Giesing, Ramersdorf, Laim, Pasing. 15 to 30 minutes to the city centre by U-Bahn or S-Bahn. Roughly 30 to 40 m² furnished at this budget.

C
S-Bahn belt

Garching, Unterschleißheim, Ismaning, Haar, Germering, Dachau. 30 to 45 minutes door to door, more space for the same rent. Fits 2 to 3 office days a week.

D
If you want more space

Up to 80 m² furnished costs well above €1,200 all-in in Munich. Before the search starts, we show you what the budget buys, so you can decide.
