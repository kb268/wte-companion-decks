Der Kandidaten-Call

15 Minuten mit einem Bewerber, der den Check gemacht und das PDF bekommen hat. Gesprochene Sätze auf Englisch, Regie auf Deutsch. Die Unterschrift fällt nicht in diesem Call, sie kommt nach Unterlagen-Prüfung und schriftlichem Angebot.

15 MINCAL.COM/KAROL-BAGINSKI/INTRO-CALL EN GESPROCHENSTAND 14.09.2026

Das Outcome: drei Ja, kein Verkauf

Erfolg ist nicht „er klingt interessiert". Erfolg ist, wenn alle drei Stufen stehen, bevor Ihr auflegt:

  1. C1 · Bedingtes JaEinwände jetzt auf dem Tisch, nicht nach dem Angebot
  2. C2 · Unterlagen + DeadlineKonkrete Liste, konkreter Tag, explizites „yes"
  3. C3 · Folgetermin gebuchtKalendereintrag entsteht noch während des Calls
0–4 MIN

Gemeinsamer Einstieg

Führung übernehmen. Langsam sprechen, viele hören Englisch als Zweitsprache.

"Hi [Name], good to meet you. I have seen your answers from the check, so I already know a bit. Here is the plan for our fifteen minutes: a few questions about your situation, then I explain how we work and what it costs, and at the end you know your next step. Sound good?"

Unsere Vorstellung, natürlich erzählt. Das ist die Deck-Story in gesprochener Form: nicht runterrattern, eher wie einem Bekannten. Sie baut das Vertrauen auf, von dem der Rest des Calls lebt.

"Before I ask you anything, let me quickly tell you who you are talking to. My name is Karol, I look after candidates and partners at WTE Companion. We are a company from Hamburg, founded by the brothers Fabian and Jakob Scholz. For years their company Charkos Europe has been bringing nurses and skilled workers to Germany: the visa, the recognition of qualifications, the relocation, all of it. In the last twelve months alone our team processed more than two hundred employment visas. German television and newspapers have reported about our work, you will find that in the presentation I sent you. And maybe the most important thing for you: we carry a German quality seal for fair recruitment, and its core rule is that candidates never pay us for job placement. So you can relax. This call is free, and I am not here to sell you anything today."

Die Weiche. Diese eine Frage entscheidet, welche Spalte unten gilt.

"Have you already completed your professional training, or do you want to learn a profession in Germany?"

4–10 MIN

Die Fragen, je nach Weg · The questions, depending on the route

Weg A · abgeschlossene Qualifikation (completed qualification)

Chancenkarte (Opportunity Card)

"Then the Chancenkarte is your route: one year in Germany to find the job, and you may work up to twenty hours a week while you search. Employers hire people who are already here."

  1. "What exactly is your qualification, and which year did you finish?"
  2. "Has a German authority ever assessed it? If not, that is normal. We check that for you."
  3. "How many years have you worked in this field since?"
  4. "Which language certificates do you hold, German or English, from which institute?" Zertifikat zählt, Selbsteinschätzung nicht
  5. "The blocked account needs about thirteen thousand euros. Is that available today, and where does it come from?"Der häufigste Antrags-Killer, nie ans Ende schieben
  6. "Have you been to Germany or Schengen before, and has a visa ever been refused?" Alte Ablehnung = andere Strategie, muss jetzt kommen
  7. "Who would come with you, and how soon could you leave?"
Weg B · Beruf erst lernen (wants to learn a profession first)

Ausbildung (Apprenticeship)

"Then the apprenticeship route is yours: up to nine months in Germany to find your training place. And it is paid training. You earn from day one, modestly, and you finish with a recognised German qualification."

  1. "How old are you exactly?"Grenze: unter 35, taggenau
  2. "Which school certificate do you hold, and does it qualify you for university in your home country?"Harte gesetzliche Bedingung
  3. "Which profession do you want to learn, and why this one?" Betriebe spüren Beliebigkeit sofort
  4. "What is your German level, and which certificate can you show?" Danach immer: "German is the make-or-break on this route. If you need more, our language partners start before you fly."
  5. "For nine months the blocked account needs about ten thousand euros. Is that available today, and where does it come from?"
  6. "Have you been to Germany or Schengen before, and has a visa ever been refused?"
  7. "Who would come with you, and how soon could you leave?"

⚠️ Keine Ausbildungsvergütungs-Zahlen nennen: sie altern und variieren je Beruf und Betrieb.

10–12 MIN

Ehrlichkeit + Preis

Wörtlich so bringen, beide Wege. Die Sperrkonto-Summe kennt er aus dem PDF und aus Deiner Frage 5, hier kommt sie bewusst ein drittes Mal, als Gesamtbild und mit dem Dreh: eigenes Geld, keine Gebühr. Preisstruktur seit 14.09. (Jakob): Paket 5.000 € + ca. 1.000 € Spesen inkl. Flug.

"Now the honest part. Nobody can promise you a job or a visa, and anyone who does is lying to you. What we do: we prepare a complete, correct file, you apply in your own name, and roughly five in six of these applications are approved. On money, the full picture, so there are no surprises: our full package is five thousand euros. That is two thousand for visa and relocation, one thousand five hundred for the accommodation search, living space guaranteed, and two thousand for integration support, from airport pickup through your first days. On top, roughly one thousand euros of third-party costs and expenses, your flight included. And separately the blocked account: about thirteen thousand euros on the Chancenkarte route, about ten thousand on the apprenticeship route. That one is not a cost and it is not for us. It stays your own money, in an account in your name, and it is paid back to you month by month after you land. If the visa is refused for reasons not caused by you, we refund our service fee. And you never pay us for finding you a job or a training place."

12–15 MIN

Das Commitment, in drei Stufen

C1

Bedingtes Ja

Holt Einwände jetzt hoch, nicht nach dem Angebot

"Let me ask you directly: if your documents check out and my written quote says exactly what I just told you, is there anything that would stop you from starting?"

Bei Zögern nachfassen, nicht überspielen: "What would need to be true for this to be a clear yes? The money, your family, the timing?"

C2

Unterlagen mit Deadline

Explizites „yes" abwarten, sonst zählt es nicht

"Here is your homework: passport, school certificate, language certificate [Weg A zusätzlich: degree and work certificates] on WhatsApp by [Wochentag]. Deal?"

C3

Folgetermin sofort buchen

Der Kalendereintrag entsteht, während Ihr sprecht

"And let's put our next call in the calendar right now. Fifteen minutes on [Tag], I am sending you the invite while we speak."

Schlusssatz: "Then that's a plan. Documents by [Tag A], we speak on [Tag B], and you will have my written quote before that call."

DANACH

Rote Flaggen + Nachlauf

Kein Geld, keine QuelleFreundlich beenden, Tür offen lassen.
Verlangt JobgarantieTrotz Klarstellung? Raus, das wird eine Beschwerde.
Verschwiegene Visa-AblehnungKommt immer raus. Dann wäre die Gebühr weg, das Vertrauen auch.
Zwei Erinnerungen, nichts geliefertEine finale Nachricht, dann Ablage.

2 Minuten nach dem Call: drei Zeilen notieren, Weg (A/B), Geldquelle, vereinbarte Deadlines. Ernsthafte Kandidaten ins CRM nur mit ID/Verweis, Dokumente bleiben in Drive.
