0–3 MIN

Gemeinsamer EinstiegOpening · Opening· 0 to 3 minutes

Führung übernehmen. Langsam sprechen, viele hören Englisch als Zweitsprache.

SAY · ENGLISH

"Hi [Name], good to meet you. I have seen your answers from the check, so I already know a bit. Here is the plan for our fifteen minutes: a few questions about your situation, then I explain how we work and what it costs, and at the end you know your next step. Sound good?"

DEUTSCH

„Hallo [Name], schön, Sie kennenzulernen. Ich habe Ihre Antworten aus dem Check gesehen, ich weiß also schon einiges. Der Plan für unsere fünfzehn Minuten: ein paar Fragen zu Ihrer Situation, dann erkläre ich, wie wir arbeiten und was es kostet, und am Ende kennen Sie Ihren nächsten Schritt. Einverstanden?"

Unsere Vorstellung, natürlich erzählt. Nicht runterrattern, eher wie einem Bekannten. Sie baut das Vertrauen auf, von dem der Rest des Calls lebt.

And maybe the most important thing for you: you never pay us for finding you a job or a training place. That is a rule we work by. So you can relax. This call is free, and I am not here to sell you anything today."

3–5 Min · Die Vorab-Fragen: wofür qualifiziert er sich?3–5 min · The pre-questions: what does he qualify for?

Kommt nach dem Kennenlernen. Leite so über: „Damit ich Dir gleich den richtigen Weg zeigen kann, stelle ich Dir vorab ein paar kurze Fragen." Zum Abhaken während des Calls. Was rot markiert ist, ist eine gesetzliche Bedingung: fehlt sie, ist der Weg zu. Passt nichts davon, sag es ehrlich und beende freundlich.

Comes after the introductions. Lead in with: "So that I can show you the right route, let me ask you a few short questions first." Tick the boxes during the call. Anything marked in red is a legal condition: if it is missing, that route is closed. If nothing fits, say so honestly and end the call warmly.

SAY · ENGLISH

"Before the money, one thing straight: nobody can promise you a job or a visa, and anyone who does is lying to you."

"What we do promise is this: we walk the whole way with you. Every step done properly, your application always in your own name, and we have gone this road with many people before you, so we know where it gets difficult."

"And the part that matters most to us is trust. We take the time to build it with you, because this is not paperwork for us, it is your future. That is exactly why we do this work."

"So, money, openly, because I do not want any surprises later. Today you pay nothing. After our call I look at your documents and send you a written quote. Only when you say yes, we start."

  • Our work: €5,000 as one package — the visa and relocation, the apartment search, and the support when you land.
  • Booked separately those three would be €5,500, so the package saves you €500.
  • You do not pay it all at once. It follows the three parts of the package:
    • €2,000 when we open your file and start the visa work
    • €1,500 when the apartment search begins
    • €1,500 before you fly, at the latest two weeks before departure
    Your €500 discount comes off the last step.
  • On top: about €1,000 of real costs that we simply pass on — translations, the degree comparison, the embassy fee, and your flight.
  • The blocked account: about €13,000. Not a fee, and it never belongs to us. It stays your money, in an account in your name. The embassy only wants to see that you can support yourself, and after you land it is paid back to you month by month.

"So what you really spend with us is five thousand plus about one thousand, spread over the months before you leave."

"And here is what happens after this call. You get three things from me by email: my presentation, so you can read all of this again in peace, and then the two documents that make up our contract, the written service order with exactly the modules and instalments I just described, and the terms of the Chancenkarte Concierge. You sign nothing today. You confirm by email only when everything in there matches what we just discussed. How does that sound to you?"
