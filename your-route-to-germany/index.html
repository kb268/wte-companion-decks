Which route fits you?

Chancenkarte · Opportunity Card

Find your job from inside Germany

For skilled workers with a degree or 2+ years of vocational training

  • One year in Germany to search, legally
  • Work up to 20 hours a week while you look, plus trial jobs
  • Language: German A1 or English B2 is enough for the card
  • Contract signed? Switch to a full work permit without leaving Germany
  • Proof of funds: about €13,000 in a blocked account, in your own name — released back to you monthly after arrival
  • ~17,500 cards issued in 2025 · about 5 in 6 applications approved
Apprenticeship search · Ausbildung

Learn a profession in Germany

For younger candidates who want German vocational training

  • Under 35 with a school certificate that qualifies for university at home
  • Up to 9 months in Germany to find your apprenticeship place
  • Requires solid German — we support you with language partners
  • Apprenticeship = paid training with a recognised German qualification
  • Proof of funds: about €10,000 in a blocked account — the amount for the 9-month stay
  • Work up to 20 hours a week while you search

Funds amounts are set by the German authorities (roughly €1,100 per month of stay in 2026) and change from time to time — we confirm the current figure on your call. Not sure which route applies? That is exactly what the first call is for.
